Using the launcher

Home for your essentials; a quick, searchable list for the rest.

Home

Home is intentionally sparse. Tap an app to open it. Tap a folder to expand its contents inline. Long-press and drag any Home item to reorder it.

Use an app's long-press menu in the app list to add or remove it from Home. Folders can also be placed on Home.

The app drawer

Swipe left from Home to open every launchable app installed on your device. The drawer is built for short trips:

  • Type in search to filter by app name.
  • Drag on the alphabet rail to jump across the list.
  • Use the list's return-to-top control after moving through a long catalog.
  • Open Settings from the app list to change its controls' position or focus behavior.

Managing apps and folders

Long-press an app to reveal management actions. You can rename its launcher label, hide it from the drawer, block it from being opened by Minimal Launcher, inspect its Android app information, or request Android's uninstall flow.

Folders

Create a folder from an app's management menu, then move other apps into it. Rename or delete a folder from Settings. Deleting a folder returns its apps to the main list.

Blocking is launcher-only. A blocked app remains installed and can still be opened through Android or another launcher. It simply will not launch through Minimal Launcher.

Appearance and Home details

Settings lets you choose system, serif, or monospace text; small, medium, or large type; and one of five accent colors. The clock, date, battery indicator, and daily usage summary can be independently shown or hidden.
