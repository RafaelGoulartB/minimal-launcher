Privacy & permissions

The launcher is designed to work on your device, without an account or network connection.

OverviewGetting startedUsing the launcherPrivacy & permissions

What stays on your device

Minimal Launcher saves its preferences using Android DataStore. This includes the Home order, custom labels, hidden and blocked app identifiers, folders, folder membership, and appearance settings. A small local cache helps the launcher render a recent app list while it refreshes.

There is no account, cloud sync, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or background service. The Android manifest does not declare the INTERNET permission.

Why permissions are used

Usage Access — optional

If you choose to show today's screen time on Home, Android's Usage Access lets the launcher calculate the device's foreground-app time for the current day. This is a system-managed permission, turned on by you in Android settings. Without it, the rest of the launcher works normally.

Package visibility

The launcher queries the device's launchable activities so it can display the apps that can be opened from Home. Android requires this declaration for app discovery on newer versions of the platform.

Uninstall confirmation

The app can open Android's own uninstall confirmation screen when you request it. Android controls the final confirmation; Minimal Launcher does not silently remove apps.

Local blocking and hiding. Hiding or blocking an app only changes Minimal Launcher's own list and launch behavior. The app remains installed and can be restored from Settings.
