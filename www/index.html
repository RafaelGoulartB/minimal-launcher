Focused Home

Keep only what earns a place on Home.

Replace the icon grid with a quiet, text-first surface. Your favorite apps and folders are easy to reach without turning the rest of your phone into visual clutter.

  • Add apps and folders to Home, then drag to reorder them.
  • Expand a folder inline without leaving your starting point.
  • Optionally show the time, date, battery level, and today's screen time.
Fast app drawer

Everything else is still one swipe away.

Search your installed apps, move through an alphabet rail, and get back to the top quickly. The list refreshes when apps are installed or removed.

  • Optional automatic focus puts the keyboard straight into search.
  • Use the alphabet rail to jump to an app section.
  • Hide or block apps from the launcher, then restore them in Settings.
Made to be yours

Simple controls, deliberate choices.

Minimal Launcher gives you a few useful ways to shape the experience without turning customization into another feed to manage.

Appearance

Choose a system, serif, or monospace typeface, three text sizes, and a restrained accent color.

Apps and folders

Group apps, rename their labels, and decide exactly which items appear on Home.

Reachable controls

Move app-list controls, choose the clock format, and only show the Home details you care about.

Three screens, one calm flow

Built around getting out of the way.

These are the three surfaces of the launcher: the quiet Home, the complete app list, and the settings that keep both in tune with you.

Your phone, your choices

Private by architecture.

Minimal Launcher stays intentionally small: no account to create, no server to contact, and no tracking SDK to remove.

No network access

The manifest does not declare internet permission. The launcher works entirely without a connection.

Optional usage access

Android's Usage Access is only used to show today's on-device foreground-app total on Home.

Local control

Home order, labels, folders, and visibility choices are stored with Android DataStore on your device.
